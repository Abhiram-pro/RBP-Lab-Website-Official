Build the foundation for an academic research-lab website: the RNA-Binding Proteins (RBP)

## COLOUR — you choose the palette

Design a professional colour scheme appropriate for a molecular biology research group at
a leading technical institute. It should read as credible, calm, and institutional — the
kind of restraint you'd expect from a university lab or a scientific journal, not from a
startup landing page or a corporate template.

Constraints:
- Predominantly light: a white or near-white page background with quiet neutral bands for
  section separation.
- One disciplined accent family used consistently for links, active states, and rules.
  Deep blues read naturally in this context, but use your judgement — pick something that
  feels considered rather than defaulting to stock Tailwind blue-600.
- Reserve one deep, saturated dark tone for the header and the hero band.
- No gradients as decoration. Colour carries hierarchy, not mood.
- Every text/background pairing must clear WCAG AA — body text at 4.5:1 minimum, and
  verify the muted secondary text too, since that is where contrast usually fails.

Tell me the palette you chose and the reasoning before you build the rest.

## TOKEN ARCHITECTURE — this structure is required

Whatever palette you pick, express it in two layers as CSS custom properties in index.css,
mapped into tailwind.config.ts:

Layer 1 — raw palette. The literal colour values, and the ONLY place a hex appears in the
entire codebase.

Layer 2 — semantic tokens that reference layer 1. Components only ever use these:

  --surface           default page background
  --surface-raised    alternating band
  --surface-sunken    alternating band, deeper
  --surface-inverse   dark band (header, hero)

  --ink               headings and body on light backgrounds
  --ink-muted         secondary text on light backgrounds
  --ink-inverse       text on dark backgrounds
  --ink-inverse-muted secondary text on dark backgrounds

  --accent            links, active states, rules
  --accent-soft       supporting accent
  --accent-light      tint for hovers and fills

  --line              hairline borders on light
  --line-inverse      hairline borders on dark

No component may contain a hardcoded colour. If I change the raw palette in layer 1, the
entire site must follow with no other edits — treat that as a hard requirement of the build.

Note on the two muted tokens: --ink-muted is only for light backgrounds and
--ink-inverse-muted only for dark. They are separate tokens on purpose. A single muted
colour cannot pass contrast on both, and muted text tuned for white becomes unreadable on
the dark hero.

## RHYTHM AND TYPE

Rhythm tokens, also as CSS custom properties:
  --content-max: 72rem
  --prose-max:   46rem
  --section-y:   clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 6.5rem)
  --gutter:      clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 3rem)

Typography: a neutral, highly legible sans for everything, plus a monospace face for small
uppercase eyebrow labels and metadata. Choose the pairing. Fluid sizes via clamp():
  h1    clamp(2.125rem, 1.6rem + 2.4vw, 3.5rem)
  h2    clamp(1.625rem, 1.35rem + 1.2vw, 2.375rem)
  body  clamp(1rem, 0.97rem + 0.15vw, 1.0625rem), line-height 1.65
Cap running text at --prose-max for a readable line length.

## VISUAL DIRECTION

Calm, spacious, content-first. Reference for information hierarchy and whitespace only:
steglelab.org. Do not copy its branding, colours, imagery, or text.

- Sections are full-bleed background bands alternating between --surface, --surface-raised
  and --surface-sunken, with content constrained to --content-max inside. The bands do the
  separating — do not wrap everything in cards.
- Every section follows: small uppercase mono eyebrow label, then heading, then an
  optional one-line lede.
- No glassmorphism, no oversized rounded cards, no decorative blobs, no scroll-triggered
  animation beyond a subtle fade-in. Border radius stays small (4–8px).
- Generous vertical space between sections.

## SHARED COMPONENTS

1. <Section> — full-bleed band. Props: tone ("base" | "raised" | "sunken" | "inverse"),
   width ("content" | "prose" | "full"), id. Applies --section-y block padding and
   --gutter inline padding, centres inner content at the right max-width.

2. <SectionHeader> — eyebrow + heading + optional lede. Prop tone ("light" | "dark")
   switching between --ink-muted and --ink-inverse-muted.

3. <PageHeader> — page-level title band: eyebrow, <h1>, lede. One per page.

4. <SiteHeader> — sticky, using --surface-inverse, 88px tall.
   Left: logo image at 56px height plus the wordmark "RBP Lab".
   Centre: nav links — Home, Research, Members, Publications, News, Equipment,
   Collaborators, Gallery (in that order).
   Right: a "Contact" button.
   The active route is marked with aria-current="page". Below lg, collapse the link row
   into a disclosure menu. On mobile, use a fixed bottom tab bar with 5 items: Home,
   Research, Members, Papers, Contact.

5. <SiteFooter> — four columns on desktop, stacking on mobile. Leave the columns empty
   for now; I'll supply the links in a later prompt.

6. <SectionNav> — sticky in-page anchor nav for long pages. Sits directly under the
   header, scrolls horizontally on mobile, and highlights the section currently in view
   using IntersectionObserver. Each item is a real <a href="#id">.

## ACCESSIBILITY — build this in from the start, not as a retrofit

- A "Skip to main content" link, visually hidden until focused, targeting <main id="main">.
- Exactly one <h1> per page; heading levels never skip.
- A visible focus ring on every interactive element (2px --accent, 3px offset).
- scroll-margin-top: 8.5rem on anchor targets so the sticky header doesn't cover them.
- A prefers-reduced-motion block that disables animation and smooth scrolling.
- Meaningful alt text on every image; alt="" on decorative ones.
- Responsive at 390px, 768px, and 1440px. Wide content scrolls inside its own container —
  the page body must never scroll horizontally.
