### Page UI

PAGE HEADER — eyebrow "RNA-Binding Proteins (RBPs) Laboratory", h1 "Contact", lede
"Enquiries about research, collaborations, and student positions are welcome."

Two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile.

LEFT — a semantic <address> element with `font-style: normal` (do not let it render
italic). A map-pin icon beside the address block, then below it a list with:
  - an envelope icon + the email as a mailto: link
  - a phone icon + the phone as a tel: link
Both must be real links, not plain text.

RIGHT — "Principal Investigator": the PI's name in semibold, her title in muted text, then
two links — an internal one to her profile route, and an external one to the institutional
page (target="_blank", rel="noopener noreferrer", with an ↗ affordance).

NO contact form. Enquiries go to the mailto link. Do not add a newsletter signup, a map
embed, or office-hours tables.

## FOOTER — keep it simple
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Deliberately minimal. Do not build a large multi-column link directory.

Three parts, stacked:

1. IDENTITY ROW — on --surface or --surface-raised.
   Left: the lab name in semibold, then the department and institute on a muted second
   line, then the email and phone as links.
   Right: a compact nav list of the main routes (Research, Members, Publications, News,
   Equipment, Collaborators, Gallery, Contact) as a simple wrapped inline list — not
   grouped into labelled columns.

2. FUNDER MARQUEE — see below.

3. BOTTOM BAR — separated by a top hairline rule. Left: "© 2026 RNA-Binding Proteins
   Laboratory, IIT Guwahati. All rights reserved." Right: a short mono funding credit line.

                                      roughly 220×120, varying widths */ ];

Build a reusable <Marquee> component:
- Render the children list TWICE back to back inside a flex track, then animate the track
  with `transform: translateX(-50%)` on a linear infinite keyframe. Duplicating is what
  makes the loop seamless — a single copy snaps back visibly at the wrap point.
- Drive duration from a `speed` prop through a CSS custom property (e.g. --duration: 28s),
  not a hardcoded value. Slow is right here — this is a credibility strip, not a banner.
- Pause on hover via `hover:[animation-play-state:paused]`.
- Constant speed the whole way. No easing, no bounce, no direction reversal.
- Fade the left and right edges with a horizontal mask-image so logos enter and exit
  softly instead of clipping at the container edge.
- Logos: fixed height (about 5rem), width auto, object-contain, so varying logo
  proportions all sit on a common baseline. Each needs alt text from the `name` field.
- `overflow: hidden` on the container, and it must not cause horizontal page scroll.
- Under prefers-reduced-motion, STOP the animation entirely and let the row be a static,
  horizontally scrollable strip. Do not merely slow it down.

## MOTION SYSTEM — subtle, applied site-wide
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Add a small, consistent set of animations across the whole site. The target is a page that
feels considered and alive, never busy. If an animation draws attention to itself, it is
wrong. This is an academic research site — motion should feel like good typesetting, not
like a product launch.

DEFINE AS TOKENS, and use them everywhere:
  --ease-out:      cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
  --duration-fast: 150ms   (hovers, focus, colour changes)
  --duration-base: 250ms   (small state changes)
  --duration-slow: 500ms   (section entrances)

WHERE MOTION IS ALLOWED:
1. Section entrance — content fades in and rises ~12px as it scrolls into view.
   --duration-slow with --ease-out. Trigger ONCE per element (do not re-animate on scroll
   back up). Use IntersectionObserver with a ~15% threshold.
2. Staggered lists — grid and list children stagger their entrance by ~40ms each, capped
   at a total of ~300ms so long lists don't crawl in for seconds.
3. Hover — links get an underline or colour shift at --duration-fast. Cards lift at most
   2px with a slightly stronger shadow. Images inside a card may scale to 1.03 maximum.
4. Focus — the focus ring appears instantly, no transition. Never animate focus.
5. Route change — a brief opacity fade on the page container, ~200ms. No slide, no scale.
6. Disclosure/menu — height or opacity at --duration-base.

FORBIDDEN:
- Scroll-linked scale, rotation, or parallax on text. This was tried here before and made
  body copy unreadable mid-scroll.
- Bounce, spring overshoot, elastic easing.
- Anything that moves more than ~16px.
- Looping or infinite animation other than the funder marquee.
- Animated counters, typewriter text, letter-by-letter reveals.
- Animation on anything above the fold at load beyond a single quiet fade.

REDUCED MOTION — a global media query that sets animation-duration and transition-duration
to ~0.01ms, sets animation-iteration-count to 1, and disables smooth scrolling. Entrance
animations must resolve to the FINAL visible state, never leaving content stuck invisible
at opacity 0. Verify this: with reduced motion on, every section must be fully readable.
