## COMPONENTS TO BUILD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

### 1. <AnchorRail>
A vertical sticky nav for desktop, pinned to the left of the content at about 12rem wide.
Each item is a real <a href="#id"> with a small dot marker on a vertical rail. The active
section's dot fills and its label goes to full weight, driven by IntersectionObserver.
Below the lg breakpoint it collapses into the horizontal scrolling <SectionNav> from the
scaffold. Respect scroll-margin-top so targets clear the sticky header.

### 2. <PipelineDiagram>
A horizontal stepper showing stages of the RNA lifecycle. Each stage: a numbered node on a
connecting horizontal rule, a label beneath, and a one-line caption below that. Nodes are
evenly distributed; the connecting line runs behind them. On mobile it rotates to a
vertical timeline rather than scrolling horizontally. Purely presentational — no click
targets, no state. Render as an ordered list for semantics.

### 3. <FocusAreaCard>
Used in the overview grid. Contains: the number in monospace, a lucide icon, the title at
h3 scale, the one-line summary, a row of keyword chips, and an anchor link "Read more ↓"
that jumps to that area's detail band further down the page. Hairline top rule, no box.
On hover the whole card lifts 2px and the icon shifts colour.

### 4. <FocusAreaBand>
The full detail treatment for one focus area, as a full-width band. Two columns on desktop:
  - narrow left: the number, the icon, and the keyword chips stacked vertically
  - wide right: eyebrow, h2, the body paragraphs at lede size, and a figure
Alternate which side the figure sits on between consecutive bands so the page doesn't read
as four identical rows. Each band carries the focus area's id as its anchor target.

### 5. <ConceptAccordion>
An accordion for the background concepts — collapsed by default except the first. Each
header: icon, eyebrow, and title, with a chevron that rotates 180° on open. Body animates
height and opacity at --duration-base. Build it on the shadcn Accordion primitive so
keyboard interaction and aria-expanded are handled. Multiple panels may be open at once.

### 6. <FigureGrid>
A responsive grid of research figures, 2 columns on tablet and 3 on desktop. Each figure:
the image in a hairline-bordered frame with a light background and object-contain padding
(these are scientific plates on white backgrounds, NOT photographs — object-cover crops
them into nonsense), a caption beneath, and a small mono uppercase meta line. Use <figure>
and <figcaption>. No lightbox.

## PAGE COMPOSITION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PAGE HEADER — <PageHeader> with eyebrow, h1, lede.

2. <AnchorRail> / <SectionNav> — anchors: Overview · Background · the four focus area
   titles · Figures

3. OVERVIEW band (id="overview") — <SectionHeader>, then a <PipelineDiagram>, then a 2×2
   grid of <FocusAreaCard>.

4. BACKGROUND band (id="background", tone="raised") — <SectionHeader>, then
   <ConceptAccordion> constrained to --prose-max.

5. FOUR <FocusAreaBand> sections — one per focus area, alternating tone between "base" and

6. FIGURES band (id="figures") — <SectionHeader>, then <FigureGrid>.

## CONSTRAINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

- Use the scaffold's <Section>, <SectionHeader>, <PageHeader> and tokens. No hardcoded
  colours. Icons come from lucide-react, resolved by name from the data.
- Every component must render correctly with an empty array, and the grid counts must not
  assume exactly 4 focus areas.
- NO scroll-linked scale, opacity, rotation or parallax on any of these sections. An
  earlier build of this page wrapped each section in a scroll-driven scale/opacity
  transform and the body text was unreadable while scrolling. Entrance fade only, once.
- No "Request collaboration", "Download PDF", "Join the lab" or contact CTAs on this page.
- Do not invent research areas, findings, or figure captions — placeholders stay fake.
